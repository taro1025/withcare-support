Privacy Policy

WithCare is an app for couples to share periods, cycles and how you feel day to day. We know that information about your cycle and your body is sensitive, so we handle only the data we need to run the app. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller responsible for your personal data is:

Rintaro Sakino, trading as Process Craft AI
1-26-10-704 Shinkawa, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 104-0033, Japan
Email: rintaro.sakino@process-craft-ai.com

WithCare is run from Japan and has no establishment in the European Union or the United Kingdom. We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK under Article 27 GDPR / UK GDPR, and we have not appointed a data protection officer. Please send any privacy question or request straight to the email address above. If we appoint a representative, we will name them here.

2. What we collect

  • Account: the user identifier from Sign in with Apple, and your name and email address if Apple shares them with us (you can hide your email with Apple’s relay address).
  • Profile and cycle settings: display name, your role (cycle tracker or supporter), usual cycle length, usual period length and the start date of your last period.
  • Daily records you choose to enter: period flow, discharge, symptoms, mood, basal body temperature, sexual activity, contraceptive pill use, ovulation and pregnancy test results, notes for your doctor, memos and the air pressure where you are.
  • Sharing: the connection with your partner, the records shared with them, reactions and notification events.
  • Community: nickname, posts, comments, likes, reports and blocks.
  • Technical data: time zone, language and region settings, push notification token, creation and update times, and logs needed to operate the service.
  • Survey answer: how you heard about WithCare, if you choose to answer during setup.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to the Supporter Plan, your subscription status and purchase history (product, dates, renewal status), linked to your WithCare account ID. If you turn off auto-renew and pick a reason in the app, we also store that reason. Payment is handled by Apple; we never receive your card details.
  • Usage information: how far you got in setup, whether the subscription screen was shown and the purchase result, whether you connected with a partner or saved a record, plus device type, iOS version and country or region. This is collected with Google Analytics for Firebase. It never includes your health records, name, email address or anything you type, and we do not collect the advertising identifier. We do not collect usage information at all on devices whose region is set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.
  • Location: only if you allow it, the app uses your approximate location (to about 3 km) to fetch the current air pressure from Apple’s WeatherKit. We store the pressure reading only, never your location.

3. Why we use your data and our legal bases

Health and sex-life data: your explicit consent

Your cycle settings and daily records are data concerning health and sex life (special categories of data under Article 9 GDPR). We process them only with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) and Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give in the app before your cycle data is first saved. We use these records only to show them to you, to calculate cycle predictions and to share them with the partner you connect. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 6). Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before. Without this consent we cannot store cycle records, which WithCare needs in order to work.

Providing WithCare: performance of a contract

We use your account, profile, sharing, community, technical and purchase data to provide WithCare under our Terms of Use: signing you in, connecting you with your partner, sending notifications, running the community, managing your subscription and answering your questions (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Safety and reliability: legitimate interests

We use technical data, reports and blocks to prevent abuse, keep the community safe, fix problems and protect the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our legitimate interest is keeping WithCare and the people who use it safe.

Improving the app: legitimate interests

Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we use usage information to see where people get stuck in the app and to make it easier to use (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use it for advertising or to identify you.

Legal obligations

Where the law requires it, for example to respond to a lawful request from an authority, we process data to comply (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

WithCare’s cycle predictions are calculated automatically from your own records and are for reference only. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who receives your data

  • Your partner: only the person you connect with using an invite code. They see the period flow, mood, symptoms, memos and air pressure of your records, your display name and cycle predictions based on your cycle settings. They do not see your basal body temperature, sexual activity, pill use, test results or notes for your doctor. Deleting your account ends the sharing and removes everything you shared.
  • Other community members: your community posts and comments are visible to other signed-in users under your nickname. Please do not post health information you would rather keep private.
  • Google (Firebase): Google LLC and its affiliates provide sign-in (Firebase Authentication), our database (Cloud Firestore), server functions (Cloud Functions), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) as our processor. Our database and server functions run in Google Cloud’s Tokyo region in Japan. We do not use Google Analytics’ advertising features (ad personalisation or the advertising identifier).
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) manages subscriptions for us as our processor and receives your WithCare account ID and your App Store purchase information.
  • Apple: Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, WeatherKit (which receives your approximate location when you add air pressure) and App Store payments. For your Apple Account and App Store purchases, Apple acts under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorities: only where we are legally required to disclose data.

We do not share your data with anyone else without your consent unless the law requires it.

5. International transfers

We are based in Japan and your data is stored in Japan. The European Commission has recognised that Japan provides an adequate level of data protection (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/419), and the United Kingdom recognises Japan as adequate under UK GDPR. We are subject to Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. For these transfers, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and RevenueCat relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for UK data). You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.

6. How long we keep data, deleting it and withdrawing consent

  • We keep your account, profile and records for as long as you have a WithCare account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This immediately deletes your profile, all your records, records shared with your partner, reactions, notifications, community posts, comments and likes, reports and blocks, your invite code, your survey answer and your sign-in account. Remaining copies in Google’s systems are removed under Google’s standard deletion process. Deleted data cannot be restored.
  • Deleting your account is how you withdraw your consent to the processing of your health data. You can also edit your records in the app at any time.
  • Usage information is kept by Google Analytics for 2 months at event level and 14 months at user level.
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat are not deleted automatically with your account. Email us and we will delete them, unless we must keep them to meet a legal obligation.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request.
  • You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings, and location access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it (Article 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Article 16);
  • have your data deleted (Article 17);
  • restrict processing (Article 18);
  • receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format and have it sent to another controller (Article 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before (Article 7(3)).

Many of these you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, email rintaro.sakino@process-craft-ai.com from the email address linked to your account, or tell us your WithCare nickname, so that we can confirm it is you. We will reply within one month.

Right to lodge a complaint: you can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work or where you think the infringement took place. You can find your authority on the European Data Protection Board’s website. In Germany, this is the data protection authority of your federal state. In the UK, it is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). In Japan, it is the Personal Information Protection Commission. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please feel free to contact us.

8. Security and children

We use access controls (only you, and the partner you connect, can read your records), encrypted connections, encryption at rest provided by Google Cloud, and separation of permissions. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

WithCare is not for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use WithCare only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Our website

These English and German pages do not use cookies or analytics. Our Japanese-language pages and articles on withcare.process-craft-ai.com use Google Analytics from Google LLC to understand which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects the pages viewed, the site or search you came from, your device and browser type and your approximate region (estimated from your IP address). It does not collect your name or email address, and nothing from the app is sent. Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off. See how Google uses this information. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
