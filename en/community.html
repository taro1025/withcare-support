Community Guidelines

These rules help us build a non-judgemental place together, where everyone can share how they feel and what their body is going through.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, or encouraging self-harm
  • Explicit sexual content, exploitation of children, or content that promotes violence or illegal activity
  • Other people’s names, contact details, schools, addresses or other personal information without their consent
  • Impersonation, scams, spam, unrelated advertising, or infringing other people’s rights
  • Spreading medical misinformation as fact, or discouraging people from seeing a doctor

Safety features

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Everyone agrees to these guidelines and the Terms of Use before using the Community.

Besides a filter that checks posts for prohibited words before they go up, you can tap “…” on any post or comment to report it or block the person. When you block someone, their posts and comments disappear from your feed straight away, and we are notified too.

We act on every report and block within 24 hours: we remove content that breaks these guidelines and remove the person who posted it from the Community.

In an emergency

If someone is in immediate danger or at risk of harming themselves or others, do not rely on reporting in the app. Contact your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US), the police, a medical service or an adult you trust.
